# 输出模板

## 快速核查

```markdown
# 结论

[未发现实质异常 / 存在需解释的问题 / 达到正式编辑调查门槛 / 官方已确认部分问题]

## 最强证据

1. [目标页码、事实、来源]
2. [目标页码、事实、来源]
3. [目标页码、事实、来源]

## 尚不能证明

- [身份、动机或网络责任边界]

## 建议下一步

- [需要的原始数据、源文件或官方核查]
```

```markdown

## 前情回顾
```

## 致编辑部材料

```markdown
Subject: Request for editorial examination of [article title / DOI]

Dear Editor,

I am writing to request an editorial examination of the article identified above.
The concerns below are limited to verifiable article-level evidence and do not
presume individual responsibility.

1. [page/figure, observation, source]
2. [page/figure, observation, source]

Suggested checks:
- obtain raw data and editable figure files;
- verify data provenance and reuse permissions;
- compare accepted and published author lists;
- review citation and peer-review records.

Sincerely,
[name]
```
